Background: for RESUMED transactions at MTU 9000 the plain-TCP receive window explains part of the gap (Paper 2, stage wp6-rwnd); Paper 1's validation does not cover MTU 9000.
Question: do full handshakes at MTU 9000 follow the stack model with the default tcp_rmem and with a raised tcp_rmem?

Measured quantity: excess latency = median of the shaped cell minus the median of the unshaped base of the same suite, depth and mTLS mode; n per cell as run.sh (default 20).

Criteria (DRAFT, to be fixed by the authors):
* P1: with the RAISED tcp_rmem the stack model's excess latency is within 2 ms of the measured excess in at least 80 % of the cells.
* P2: with the DEFAULT tcp_rmem at least 10 % of the cells differ from the model by more than 0.5 RTT (the receive window matters at MTU 9000), and every such cell has a measured excess ABOVE the model.
* P3 (exploratory): the cells that differ are those where the server flight exceeds the advertised window (peak bytes in flight from the plain-TCP trace of the same run, slack.csv).
Not covered: tickets (full handshakes without tickets, as Paper 1).
